Retries, from the idea to the numbers

The idea

A retry trades extra load for a better chance of success. Hover a level to preview it, or click to keep it.

Try again, because many failures are brief. Try again up to three times, waiting longer each time, and only for failures that can pass, such as timeouts or 503, never for a 400.

What can go wrong

If every client retries on the same schedule, they arrive together: a retry storm. Random jitter spreads retries over time.

With jitter

  • Retries spread out

Without jitter

  • A retry storm can keep the service down
