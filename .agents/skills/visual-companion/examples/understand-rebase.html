How an interactive rebase lands your commits

Rebase replays your commits onto a new base, one at a time, and stops on the first conflict. Nothing is lost: the old commits stay reachable from the reflog.

Like rewriting a shopping list from a different starting point: each item is copied across in order, and you stop when the paper underneath disagrees.

base: the commit your work is replayed onto. HEAD: the commit you are standing on right now — detached means it has no branch name.

Both edits touch the same paragraph. Git writes both sides into the file and waits; nothing else moves until you decide.
