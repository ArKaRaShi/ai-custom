## Limits

- The sources support principles, not a sequence. Concreteness fading does not generalize: Kokkonen and Schalk 2021 found it may fail in some domains, and Kaminski et al. 2008 favored abstract examples in math. The rules therefore have no fixed order.
- No study I found tests these rules on adults with ADHD, or on a specific technical domain.
